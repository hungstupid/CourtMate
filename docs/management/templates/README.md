# Reusable management templates — PA0 through Final

**Templates are never evidence of completed work.** Copy and fill these files when the team actually does the work; keep the original templates intact.

- [WEEKLY_STATUS_TEMPLATE.md](WEEKLY_STATUS_TEMPLATE.md) — weekly W2–W10 status, progress, blockers and risks.
- [MEETING_MINUTES_TEMPLATE.md](MEETING_MINUTES_TEMPLATE.md) — meeting decisions/actions/TA feedback.
- [DECISION_LOG_TEMPLATE.md](DECISION_LOG_TEMPLATE.md) — decisions and changes with rationale, alternatives and impacted baselines.
- [RISK_REGISTER_TEMPLATE.md](RISK_REGISTER_TEMPLATE.md) — probability × loss, exposure, mitigation and contingency.
- [WORK_PACKAGE_TEMPLATE.md](WORK_PACKAGE_TEMPLATE.md) — WBS leaf task owner, effort, dependencies and Done evidence.

**Where filled files go:** reports → `../weekly/`, minutes → `../meetings/`, WBS/risks/decisions → `../planning/`, individual evidence → `../contributions/`; final PA copies → `../../../pa/PAx/`.
